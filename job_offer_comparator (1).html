Financial Methodology & Mathematical Specification

1. Commute Duration (Hrs) (5 − Remote Days) × 50 wks × (Commute Mins ÷ 60)
2. Commute Cash Cost (5 − Remote Days) × 50 wks × $20.00/day
3. Effective Compensation Time Value = Hours × $25/hr
Effective = Gross Base − Transit Cash − Time Value

* Note: Effective Hourly Rate is computed as (Base Salary − Transit Cash Cost) ÷ (2,000 Annual Work Hours + Annual Commute Hours), preventing double-counting of commute time penalties while standardizing across total committed hours.
